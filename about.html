Who We Are

Great cinematic sound design is not just about recording noise; it is about capturing the unseen psychological layers of a story. Founded by Kampala-based sound designer and location recordist Etiang Joan, Sonic Pulse was born out of a passion for bringing international technical fidelity to African narratives.

With extensive experience tracking multi-channel audio in unpredictable environments and turning complex scripts into rich sonic realities, Sonic Pulse has built a reputation for bulletproof reliability from set to studio.

"We respect the silence, so we can control the sound."
